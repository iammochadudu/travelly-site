What makes Travelly different

Six things, in the order they matter.

01

Real prices, not estimates

Live flight and hotel offers, checked against your actual dates and budget, not generic averages.

02

Your budget is the limit, always

Travelly won't suggest a plan that breaks your budget. If something doesn't fit, you'll know immediately, not at checkout.

03

Compare trip versions side by side

See what changes if you shift budget between accommodation, food, activities, and transport — instantly, with no re-planning.

04

Real places, not filler

Restaurants, hotels, and attractions sourced with actual ratings, reviews, and photos — not AI-invented suggestions.

05

A full itinerary, day by day

Not just a budget number — a real plan you can follow, built around what you can actually afford.

06

Ask anything

Built-in AI chat for trip questions, grounded in your actual budget and itinerary, not generic travel advice.

BOOKING IMPORTS

Four people, four booking PDFs, one budget

Groups rarely book together. Add one entry per booking — flights and stays each get their own screen — and say which travelers it covers. Every total is summed into the itinerary and the budget breakdown, and each entry keeps the name of whoever imported it. Drop the PDF and the route, dates, reference and paid total are read out of it, with a confidence label to check before saving.

GROUP TRIPS

One plan, four people, no spreadsheet

Shared itineraries, group chat, expense splitting, and optional live location sharing for travelers moving as a group. Roles and soft-locking mean two people editing the same day don't overwrite each other.

LIVE LOCATION

Pinned to the top of the conversation

Live location lives inside the trip chat, not in a separate screen: a map pinned above the messages, every sharing traveler’s pin echoing as their position updates. Tap it to start or stop your own — 15 min, 1 hr or 8 hr, opt-in per person.
